Research & interests

No. 01·AGalveston, TX

Research focus

Vaccine correlates of protection. I'm building a scalable single-cell multi-omics pipeline, validated in NHP vaccination models, to move toward human-relevant immune correlates of vaccine efficacy.

No. 01·Bin silico

Computational approach

Machine learning and biostatistics on high-dimensional biological and clinical data, from single-cell transcriptomics to electronic health records, propensity score matching, and post-vaccination outcome prediction.

No. 01·CSan Antonio, TX

Immunology roots

Mucosal immunity and host–pathogen interaction: lymphotoxin β-receptor signaling and IFN-γ–driven intestinal pathology in Campylobacter-induced colitis.

No. 01·DEHR cohorts

Clinical real-world data

Using EHR cohorts to ask epidemiological questions, like the relative risk of sudden sensorineural hearing loss after infectious disease, or reactions after yellow fever vaccination.
